# Publish kit — the value of a premortem before a product launch

*Thesis:* A 60-minute premortem before launch is the highest-ROI risk move there is: it surfaces the failure reasons your team won't say out loud while they're still cheap to fix.

*Audience:* Product leaders, founders, and launch teams about to ship.
