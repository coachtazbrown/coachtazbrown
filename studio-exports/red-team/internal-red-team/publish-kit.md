You don't need to hire a firm to red-team your strategy. You can build the capability in-house.

1. Give it a charter — its job is to make every big plan survive a structured attack before you commit.
2. Protect its independence — a red team that reports to the plan's owner pulls its punches.
3. Give it a cadence + method — run a key assumptions check and a premortem on every major bet, every quarter.

That's what turns red teaming from a one-off stunt into an owned capability.
