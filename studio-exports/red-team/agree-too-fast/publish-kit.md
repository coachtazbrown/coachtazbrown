```
Why the best leadership teams distrust fast agreement

Fast, comfortable consensus on a hard call usually isn't alignment — it's groupthink (Janis, 1972). Healthy teams generate friction and resolve it; groupthink teams skip to smooth.
```

**Post copy:**

```
If your leadership team agreed fast and everyone felt good about it — be careful.

Comfortable consensus usually isn't alignment. It's groupthink: the drive for harmony quietly overriding a real look at the alternatives (Janis, 1972).

The danger isn't that people disagree and hide it. It's that they stop generating the disagreement at all. Smooth rooms miss things.

The fix isn't hoping someone speaks up — it's assigning it. Give one person the job of arguing the other side. Make dissent a role, not a personality.

In your last big decision — who was actually responsible for arguing against it?
```
