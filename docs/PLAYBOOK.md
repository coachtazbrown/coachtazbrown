# The Taz Brown Strategies Playbook

Strategy document for the practice, not the codebase. Living doc — edit as you learn.

---

## 1. The bet

Every founder and leadership team makes irreversible decisions on assumptions nobody was paid to attack. They don't lack intelligence — they lack a structure that makes dissent cheap and an outsider credible enough to run it. That outsider is the practice. The product is **installed adversarial thinking**, not a slide deck.

The whole canon already exists, 1587 → today (see `lib/redteam-knowledge.ts`). The moat isn't the techniques — they're public. The moat is being the credible external party who runs them, coaches the leader who owns them, and makes the client's own people produce the finding.

---

## 2. The value ladder

Four rungs. Each is engineered so the client asks for the next — we grow on wins, not sales pressure.

| Rung | Offer | Why it converts |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Land** | 90-min Key Assumptions Check or live Premortem on one real decision | Low friction, undeniable, ends with a finding they can't unsee |
| **2 — Diagnose** | Decision-quality audit on the last three big bets | Reframes a one-off win as a systemic gap |
| **3 — Cadence** | Quarterly war-game / Team B tied to the roadmap | Recurring revenue anchored to their planning rhythm |
| **4 — Install & Partner** | Stand up their internal red cell; Taz as standing partner + the leader's coach | Multi-year, highest-margin, referral-generating |

The math to a $1M recurring practice is **fewer clients up the ladder**, not more clients at Land. ~6 clients at Cadence + 3 at Install & Partner clears it.

---

## 3. The operating principles

These are non-negotiable and they are the brand:

1. **Coach, don't consult.** The client reaches the finding themselves — they defend what they conclude and forget what they're told.
2. **Name the lineage.** Devil's Advocate 1587, Premortem 2007. Origin is credibility.
3. **Cheapest move first.** A 90-minute assumptions check over a war-game when it solves it — even though the war-game bills more. Credibility compounds into more clients than any invoice.
4. **Diagnose structure, never people.** Clients defend their team and fire the consultant who attacks character.
5. **A finding with no owner was theatre.** Every engagement ends with dangerous items converted to owned actions with dates, and a debrief on the calendar.

---

## 4. The growth engine

The debrief is the bridge: a Land premortem's 30-day debrief is, by design, the Diagnose pitch. A Diagnose audit's circulated artifacts are the Cadence pitch. The Install & Partner client's cleared wins become case studies and warm intros — which become the next Land clients. The loop closes; the practice compounds.

The Red Teaming Partner (this codebase) is the force multiplier: it lets one principal run the full canon at depth across the whole book, and never lets a recommendation end without pointing at the next rung.

---

## 5. Yearly

New technique entries as the canon evolves (AI red teaming analogues, scenario war-gaming variants). The method is the asset — keep it current, keep it cited, keep it earliest → latest.
